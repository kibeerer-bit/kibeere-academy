Password Managers: Protect Your Online Accounts

Discover how password managers help you create, store and manage strong passwords. Learn about online account security and explore popular password management services.

What Is a Password Manager?

A password manager is a digital tool that securely stores your passwords and helps you manage login information for websites, applications and online accounts.

Instead of remembering many different passwords, you can use one strong master password to access your password vault. Many password managers can also generate strong and unique passwords for your accounts.

Why Use a Password Manager?

  • Create strong and unique passwords.
  • Reduce the risk of reusing passwords across websites.
  • Store login information in an encrypted vault.
  • Save time with automatic form filling.
  • Access passwords across supported devices.
  • Identify weak or reused passwords with supported security tools.

How to Choose a Password Manager

Before selecting a password manager, consider these factors:

  • Security: Understand its encryption and account recovery protections.
  • Privacy: Read its privacy policy and understand how your data is handled.
  • Price: Compare free and paid features.
  • Compatibility: Check support for your phone and computer.
  • Ease of use: Choose a service you can use confidently.
  • Recovery: Understand what happens if you lose access to your master password.

Important Password Security Tips

  • Never share your master password.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication where available.
  • Use a unique password for every important account.
  • Keep your devices and applications updated.
  • Be careful with suspicious emails and login links.
  • Keep account recovery information secure.

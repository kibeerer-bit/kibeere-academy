VPN Reviews and Comparisons

Understand Virtual Private Networks, explore popular VPN providers and learn how to make informed decisions about your online privacy.

Understanding Virtual Private Networks (VPNs)

A Virtual Private Network (VPN) creates an encrypted connection between your device and a VPN server. It can help protect your internet traffic on public Wi-Fi networks and hide your public IP address from websites you visit.

VPN services are useful privacy tools, but they do not make you completely anonymous or protect you from every online threat. You should still use strong passwords, multi-factor authentication and safe browsing practices.

Why Consider Using a VPN?

  • Encrypt internet traffic between your device and the VPN server.
  • Add protection when using public Wi-Fi.
  • Help conceal your public IP address from websites.
  • Provide access to different VPN server locations.
  • Support privacy when browsing on supported devices.
Security reminder: A VPN does not replace antivirus software, secure passwords, software updates or responsible internet use.

VPN Services Worth Comparing

The following providers offer different features and subscription options. Compare their official terms before choosing a service.

1. Proton VPN

Proton VPN offers both free and paid plans. Its free plan includes unlimited data, no advertisements and one simultaneous device connection. Some advanced features are reserved for paid plans.

Consider it if: You want to explore a reputable free VPN option for everyday privacy.

Things to consider: The free plan has fewer server choices and features than the paid plan.

2. NordVPN

NordVPN offers paid VPN subscriptions with features such as encrypted connections, multiple VPN protocols, a kill switch and additional privacy tools. Its support documentation lists support for up to 10 devices on one subscription.

Consider it if: You want to compare a paid VPN service with additional security and privacy features.

Things to consider: Check subscription renewal prices, available plans and device limits before purchasing.

Visit NordVPN

3. Surfshark

Surfshark provides paid VPN services and advertises unlimited simultaneous device connections. Its plans and included features can vary, so check the current subscription details.

Consider it if: You want to compare a service that supports multiple devices under one subscription.

Things to consider: Review renewal prices, supported features and refund conditions before subscribing.

Free VPN vs Paid VPN

Free VPN Services

Free VPN plans can be useful for beginners who want basic privacy protection without paying a subscription. However, some free services restrict server selection, device connections or advanced features.

Paid VPN Services

Paid VPN subscriptions may provide more server locations, additional security features, wider device support and customer assistance. Compare the total subscription cost and renewal terms before buying.

How to Choose the Right VPN

  1. Check the provider's privacy policy and logging practices.
  2. Look for independent security audits.
  3. Check supported devices and operating systems.
  4. Compare server locations and connection speeds.
  5. Understand subscription renewal and refund conditions.
  6. Research the company's reputation and customer support.

VPN Safety Tips

  • Download VPN applications from official websites or trusted app stores.
  • Avoid unknown free VPN applications that lack clear privacy policies.
  • Keep your VPN application updated.
  • Use multi-factor authentication where available.
  • Do not use a VPN to carry out illegal activities.
